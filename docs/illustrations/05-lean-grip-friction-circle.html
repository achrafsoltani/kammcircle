Model: point mass, steady speed, flat road. Lean = atan(v² ÷ g·r); grip used = √(lateral g² + braking g²) ÷ μ; grip-limit lean = atan(μ); max speed = √(√(μ² − b²)·g·r). Real bikes lean a few degrees more than this because the contact point moves off the tyre centreline (see docs/physics.md in the kammcircle repository). μ presets come from src/kammcircle/data/surfaces.json.
